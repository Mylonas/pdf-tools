Privacy Policy

Last updated:

🔒 Your PDF and image files are processed entirely in your browser and are never uploaded to any server.

Your files never leave your device

meldpdf runs every operation — merging, splitting, converting and rotating — locally inside your web browser using JavaScript and WebAssembly. The files you choose are read into your browser's memory, processed on your own device, and offered back to you as a download. They are never transmitted to us or to any third party. We have no server that receives, stores or sees your documents.

Information we collect

We do not require an account and we do not ask for your name, email or any personal information to use the tools. We do not store the contents of your files.

Like most websites, we may collect standard, non-identifying technical information through analytics and advertising partners, such as your approximate region, browser type, device type, and which pages you visit. This helps us understand which tools are useful and keep the site running.

Cookies and advertising

This site may display advertising to keep the tools free. When advertising is enabled, third-party vendors — including Google — may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can opt out of third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info/choices.
  • Third-party vendors and ad networks may also use cookies to serve ads on this site.

Analytics

We may use privacy-respecting analytics to measure aggregate traffic. Analytics data is used only in aggregate and is not used to identify individual visitors.

Local storage

The site may store small preferences (such as your last-used tool) in your browser's local storage. This never contains your file contents and never leaves your device.

Children's privacy

meldpdf is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Because we do not collect personal data directly, there is generally nothing for us to delete on request. For data handled by advertising partners, please use the opt-out links above or the privacy controls of the relevant provider.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the "last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us at hello@meldpdf.com.
