MeldPDF Android App — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026.

🔒 Every PDF operation in the MeldPDF app runs entirely on your device. Your documents are never uploaded to us or to any server.

This policy explains how the MeldPDF Android app (package com.mikmy.meldpdf), published by Black Falcon Studios, handles your data. It is separate from the privacy policy for the meldpdf.com website.

What the app collects

Nothing that leaves your device. Every operation — merging, splitting, compressing, converting, OCR, signing, adding page numbers or watermarks, stripping metadata, and protecting or unlocking PDFs — runs locally on your device. Your documents are never uploaded to us or to any server. Files you open are read only to perform the operation you asked for, and the result is saved only where you choose to save or share it. The app has no accounts, no login, and no analytics of our own.

Advertising

The app displays ads through Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google's advertising SDK may collect and process your device's advertising ID and related device information. This is standard for ad-supported apps and is governed by Google's own policies:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Google AdMob & Privacy

In the EEA and UK, the app shows a consent request (Google's User Messaging Platform) before serving personalised ads, as required by law. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation at any time in your device's Google settings.

Permissions

The app requests access to the specific files you pick, through Android's standard file picker, only to process them. It uses the internet solely to request and display ads.

Children

The app is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the "last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us at contact@meldpdf.com or via the contact page.
