How to combine bank statements into one PDF

Mortgage brokers, landlords, visa offices and lenders often ask for "the last 3–6 months of statements as a single PDF". Here's the quick, private way to do it.

Open the Merge tool →

Why this comes up so often

Banks usually let you download one statement per month, so you end up with three, six or twelve separate PDFs. Almost every application wants them combined into one file, in date order, for easy reading. That's a simple merge — but bank statements are about the most sensitive document you own, so where you merge them matters.

Step by step

  1. Download each monthly statement from your online banking as a PDF.
  2. Open the meldpdf Merge tool and add all of them.
  3. Drag them into date order (oldest to newest is usually expected).
  4. Click Merge PDFs — the combined file downloads as one document.

Keep your financial data private

This is exactly the situation where you should not use a tool that uploads your files. meldpdf merges everything inside your browser — your statements never leave your device and are never sent to any server. There's nothing to be stored, logged or leaked. (More on why that matters: is it safe to upload PDFs?)

Handy extras

  • Only need certain months? Extract specific pages first, then merge.
  • A statement scanned sideways? Rotate it before merging.
  • Statements as images instead of PDFs? Convert them to PDF first.

Frequently asked questions

What order should the statements be in?

Unless told otherwise, oldest to newest reads most naturally and is what most reviewers expect.

Can I combine statements from different banks?

Yes — add them all and arrange them however the application asks.

Is my banking data safe here?

Yes. Merging happens locally in your browser; nothing is uploaded. See our privacy policy.
