Delete pages from a PDF

Need to drop a blank page, a duplicate, or a section you don't want to share? Delete pages from any PDF right here — your file never leaves your device.

Type the pages you want gone (single numbers or ranges like 2, 5-7) and download a clean copy. Everything runs in your browser, so even sensitive documents stay private.

How it works

  1. Add your PDF. Drop your PDF in or click to choose it — it stays on your device.
  2. Enter the pages to delete. Use single numbers and ranges, e.g. 2, 5-7.
  3. Download. Save the new PDF with those pages removed.

When to delete pages (and when to split instead)

Deleting is the right choice when you want to keep most of a document but drop a few specific pages — a blank sheet left by a scanner, a fax cover page, a duplicate, or an appendix you don't want to share. If instead you want to keep only a small selection and discard the rest, the split / extract tool is faster: you list the pages to keep rather than the ones to remove. Both produce a fresh PDF and leave your original untouched.

Common reasons people remove PDF pages

  • Bank statements & invoices — trim a statement down to the months a landlord, bank or visa office actually asked for.
  • Scanned documents — drop the blank back of a double-sided scan, or a stray separator sheet.
  • Contracts & reports — remove an internal appendix or a signature page before circulating a draft.
  • Slide handouts — cut the title or notes pages before printing.

Getting the page numbers right

Pages are counted from 1, in the order they appear in the file. Enter single pages and ranges together, separated by commas — for example 1, 4, 9-12 removes page 1, page 4, and pages 9 through 12. Numbering always refers to the original document, so you don't have to recalculate as pages are removed. If you're not sure which pages to cut, open the PDF in any viewer first and note the numbers.

What deleting does — and doesn't do

Removing a page deletes that page and everything drawn on it, and the new file is usually a little smaller. It is not the same as compressing (the remaining pages keep their full quality — see compress a PDF) and it is not redaction: text or data on the pages you keep is unchanged. Any bookmarks or links that pointed at a removed page will no longer resolve.

Frequently asked questions

Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

No. Pages are removed in your browser and the file is never uploaded.

How do I delete a range of pages?

Enter a range like 5-7, and combine several with commas, e.g. 2, 5-7, 10.

Can I keep only a few pages instead?

Yes — use the Split / Extract tool to keep just the pages you want.
