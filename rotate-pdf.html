How to rotate a PDF and keep it that way

Fix sideways or upside-down scans and save a permanently rotated PDF.

🔒 Rotation is applied in your browser — your PDF is never uploaded.
Open the Rotate tool →

Step by step

  1. Open the Rotate tool and add your PDF.
  2. Choose how far to turn it: 90° right, 180°, or 90° left.
  3. Click Rotate PDF. Every page turns and the result downloads as rotated.pdf.

Why rotation "sticks" here

Turning a page in a PDF viewer often only changes how it looks on screen — reopen the file and it's sideways again. meldpdf writes the rotation into the document itself, so the pages stay the right way up everywhere: in email previews, on other devices, and when printed.

Common fixes

  • A scanner or phone that saved pages in landscape by mistake.
  • A fax or scan that came through upside down (use 180°).
  • Photos of documents captured at the wrong angle.

Frequently asked questions

Can I rotate only some pages?

The tool currently rotates all pages together. To rotate just a few, first extract those pages, rotate them, then merge them back in.

Does rotating reduce quality?

No. Rotation only changes page orientation metadata; text and images are untouched.

Is my file uploaded?

No. Everything happens locally in your browser. See our privacy policy.
