How it works

  1. Add your PDF. Drop it in or click to choose it.
  2. Set the watermark. Type the text and choose the opacity.
  3. Download. Save the watermarked PDF.

What a watermark is good for

A watermark stamps a word diagonally across every page — DRAFT, CONFIDENTIAL, COPY, SAMPLE, or your own name or company. It signals status and ownership at a glance, discourages casual reuse of a document, and makes it obvious when a printed copy is not the final version.

Common uses

  • Drafts under review — mark a version DRAFT so no one mistakes it for the signed copy.
  • Sensitive documents — a CONFIDENTIAL stamp sets expectations before anyone reads on.
  • Samples and proofs — share a preview without giving away a clean, reusable file.
  • Attribution — put your name or brand across pages you send out.

Choosing the opacity

Light opacity places a faint mark behind the text that stays readable — good for long documents. Medium is a sensible default. Strong makes the watermark dominant, which suits short notices where the status matters more than easy reading. Whatever you choose, the original text underneath is untouched.

A watermark is a deterrent, not a lock

Be clear-eyed about what a watermark does: it's a visual mark, not security. A determined person could re-render or crop it. If you need to actually restrict who can open or change a file, add a password with protect a PDF — and you can do both. This tool currently adds diagonal text across all pages; it runs entirely in your browser and, of course, meldpdf never adds a watermark of its own.

Frequently asked questions

Is my PDF uploaded?

No. The watermark is applied in your browser; the file is never uploaded.

Does meldpdf add its own watermark?

Never. The only watermark on your PDF is the text you choose.

Can I make the watermark subtle?

Yes — pick the Light opacity for a faint background mark, or Strong for a bold one.
