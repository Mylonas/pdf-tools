Add page numbers to a PDF

Stamp clean page numbers onto every page of a PDF — pick the corner, the starting number and the style. Runs in your browser, so your file stays private.

Choose where the numbers sit (bottom centre, left or right), what number to start from, and the format (1, 1 / N, or Page 1). Then download the numbered PDF.

How it works

  1. Add your PDF. Drop it in or click to choose it.
  2. Pick the style. Choose position, start number and format.
  3. Download. Save the PDF with page numbers stamped on every page.

Why add page numbers

Numbered pages make a document easier to navigate, cite and print. They matter most for anything that will be read on paper or referenced by page: court and legal filings, tender and grant submissions, printed handouts, manuals, contracts that say "see page 12", and long reports that go out for review.

Choosing a format and position

You can stamp a plain number (1), a number with the total (1 / 10), or a labelled form (Page 1). The "1 / N" style is the friendliest for print, because a reader can immediately see how much is left. Numbers can sit in the bottom centre, left or right — pick a corner that won't collide with an existing footer, logo or margin note.

Starting from the right number

Set the starting number to handle front matter: begin at 0 or a later page if a cover or contents page shouldn't count, or start at a higher number to continue the sequence of a document that follows another. The number you set is applied to the first page and increments from there.

What this tool is — and isn't

It draws clean numbers as a text layer on top of each page, so they're crisp and selectable. It is deliberately simple: it doesn't do full legal Bates numbering (prefixes and zero-padded sequences across many files), and it doesn't detect numbers a document may already have — so remove any old numbering first if you don't want two sets. Everything is applied in your browser and the file is never uploaded.

Frequently asked questions

Is my PDF uploaded?

No. Page numbers are drawn in your browser and the file is never uploaded.

Can I start numbering from a page other than 1?

Yes — set the start number (you can even start at 0 for a cover page).

Can I show “Page 1 of 10”?

Yes — choose the 1 / N format to show each page out of the total.
