Organize PDF pages — reorder, rotate, delete

See every page as a thumbnail, drag them into the order you want, rotate the sideways ones and remove the pages you don't need — then save. All in your browser.

Drag pages to reorder, use the rotate and remove buttons on each thumbnail, and download the rebuilt PDF. Your document is processed entirely on your device.

How it works

  1. Add your PDF. Its pages load as draggable thumbnails.
  2. Rearrange. Drag pages to reorder, tap rotate to turn a page, or remove pages you don't want.
  3. Save. Download the reorganised PDF.

Frequently asked questions

Is my PDF uploaded?

No. Thumbnails are rendered and the PDF is rebuilt in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

Can I reorder and delete at the same time?

Yes — drag to reorder and mark pages for removal, then save once.

Does rotating a page here stick?

Yes. The rotation is saved into the downloaded PDF, not just shown on screen.
