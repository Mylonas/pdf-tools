Organize PDF pages — reorder, rotate, delete

See every page as a thumbnail, drag them into the order you want, rotate the sideways ones and remove the pages you don't need — then save. All in your browser.

Drag pages to reorder, use the rotate and remove buttons on each thumbnail, and download the rebuilt PDF. Your document is processed entirely on your device.

How it works

  1. Add your PDF. Its pages load as draggable thumbnails.
  2. Rearrange. Drag pages to reorder, tap rotate to turn a page, or remove pages you don't want.
  3. Save. Download the reorganised PDF.

What you can do here

This is a visual page organiser: every page of your PDF becomes a thumbnail you can rearrange. You can drag pages into a new order, rotate a page that was scanned sideways, and remove pages you don't want — then save one clean PDF with all of those changes applied at once. It's the tool to reach for when a single job needs more than one of those actions.

Everyday uses

  • Fix a messy scan — put pages that came out of the feeder in the wrong order back where they belong.
  • Assemble a document — after merging several PDFs, drag the sections into the final running order.
  • Rotate mixed orientations — turn the one landscape table in a portrait report the right way up.
  • Move an appendix — shift supporting pages to the back before sharing.

Reorder, rotate or just delete?

If all you need is to remove pages, delete pages is quicker. If you only need to turn every page the same way, rotate a PDF does that in one click. Organize is for when you want to combine actions — reorder and rotate and drop a page — and see the result as you go.

Good to know

Drag-and-drop works best on a desktop or laptop; on a phone you can still rotate and remove pages. Rotation is baked into the saved file, not just shown on screen, so the downloaded PDF opens the right way up everywhere. As with every meldpdf tool, the thumbnails are rendered on your device and the file is never uploaded.

Frequently asked questions

Is my PDF uploaded?

No. Thumbnails are rendered and the PDF is rebuilt in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

Can I reorder and delete at the same time?

Yes — drag to reorder and mark pages for removal, then save once.

Does rotating a page here stick?

Yes. The rotation is saved into the downloaded PDF, not just shown on screen.
