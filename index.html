Free browser-based PDF toolkit

Unlike most online PDF converters, meldpdf processes everything locally in your browser with JavaScript and WebAssembly. Your documents are never sent to a server, so it's safe for contracts, payslips, IDs and other sensitive files. No account, no email, no watermark, no file-size games.
